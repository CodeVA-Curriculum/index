This design menu shows some options that you can choose from when you're thinking about a story you might create with Twine. I chose my goals based on my story idea--an evil whale does battle with an even evil-er captain.

I haven't gone over any Twine coding concepts yet, but it's a good idea to start thinking about the kinds of stories you might make using basic Twine programs. These project options should help you think about what events will take place in the stories that *you* write. Here's the design menu I completed for my story, *The Whale*:

![My completed design menu](TODO:)

My story is a re-telling of *Moby Dick*, in which a morally compromised sea captain pursues an elemental whale so doggedly he dooms his crew and ship forever. I think the reader will end up making decisions in the story during the final moments of the novel, and the story will have a couple of different good and bad endings depending on the path the reader chooses.

You can use the blank spots in the design menu to make up your own goals if the ones that I provided aren't interesting or don't suit the story you'd like to make. Get creative!
